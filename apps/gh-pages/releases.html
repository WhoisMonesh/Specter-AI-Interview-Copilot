🚀 Release Highlights & What's New in v5.0.0

  • 100% Free & Pure BYOM (Bring Your Own Model): Complete removal of paywalls, subscription tiers, Stripe checkout servers, and hardcoded personal keys. Directly plug in free or paid keys from Google Gemini (2.5 Flash / Pro), Groq (Llama 3.3 70B), OpenAI (GPT-4o), Anthropic Claude, OpenRouter, and NVIDIA NIM.
  • Zero Screen Share Detection: Hardware-level display masking keeps the floating copilot 100% invisible to Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, and Discord on both macOS (NSWindowSharingNone) and Windows (WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE).
  • Stealth Dock & Taskbar Camouflage (Locked AI Mode): Hides the application icon completely from the macOS Dock and Windows/Linux taskbars. Disguises window title and branding into Notes, Calculator, Google Chrome Helper, or completely blank.
  • Sub-Second OCR & Region Snip: Instant fullscreen OCR solve (⌘⇧S / Ctrl+Shift+S) or custom drag-and-drop region capture (⌘⇧X / Ctrl+Shift+X) to solve DSA problems and system design questions.
  • Dual-Channel Audio Loopback Transcription: Concurrently captures interviewer speech through system loopback and candidate microphone to provide real-time anticipatory speaking outlines.
  • Candidate Profile Grounding: Injects your target role, years of experience, and resume summary into behavioral and STAR questions.

🍎 macOS Installation

Mount the downloaded .dmg and drag Specter AI to Applications. If Gatekeeper prompts on first open:

  1. Right-click Specter AI Copilot.app and select Open, or
  2. Run in Terminal: xattr -d com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Specter\ AI\ Copilot.app

🪟 Windows Installation

Run the Setup.exe installer or launch the standalone portable .exe directly. If Windows SmartScreen appears:

  1. Click More info.
  2. Click Run anyway to start the undetectable overlay.

🐧 Linux Installation

For universal AppImage or Debian/Ubuntu .deb package:

  1. Make executable: chmod +x Specter.AI.Copilot-5.0.0.AppImage
  2. Or install deb: sudo dpkg -i specter-ai-copilot_5.0.0_amd64.deb
